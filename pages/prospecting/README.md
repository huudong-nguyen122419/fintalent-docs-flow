## What each activity means

### `1.01` Build target account lists and campaign segments

Create focused lists by ICP, geography, relationship, trigger and commercial relevance.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted list building  
**Note:** Revenue leadership sets target-account priorities; SDR builds and works the resulting account lists.

### `1.02` Prioritize accounts and queue next actions

Order the daily work queue so the highest-value next action is clear.

**Automation target:** Rule-based and AI-assisted prioritization  
**Note:** V1 can remain deterministic rather than score-heavy.

### `1.03` Research target accounts

Understand the company, ownership, current situation, staffing model and likely needs.

**Automation target:** AI research brief

### `1.04` Identify commercial triggers

Detect events that create urgency or a likely need for external finance, M&A or transformation support.

**Automation target:** Automated signal monitoring

### `1.05` Identify and validate decision-makers

Find the most relevant buyer, influencer or relationship owner for the opportunity.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted contact recommendation

### `1.06` Prepare personalized outreach

Create a relevant message based on the account, trigger, relationship and likely business need.

**Automation target:** AI-generated draft with human review  
**Note:** SDR owns standard personalized outreach; KAM can support strategic-account and senior-stakeholder messaging.

### `1.07` Execute multichannel outreach

Contact prospects through email, LinkedIn, calls, events, referrals and Fintalent touchpoints.

**Automation target:** Sequence and send assistance  
**Note:** SDR executes the standard cadence; KAM may directly engage priority senior stakeholders where appropriate.

### `1.08` Manage follow-up cadence and contact progression

Follow up at appropriate intervals without contacting multiple people at the same company simultaneously.

**Automation target:** Automated reminders and sequences  
**Note:** Automation supports reminders and sequencing; SDR remains accountable for the follow-up.

### `1.09` Triage replies and qualify initial interest

Categorize replies, respond appropriately and determine whether a commercial conversation is warranted.

**Automation target:** AI reply classification and response drafting

### `1.10` Book discovery call and hand over context

Schedule the meeting and transfer account research, outreach history, trigger and initial need to the next owner.

**Automation target:** Automated scheduling and handover summary  
**Note:** SDR owns meeting booking and the structured handover; KAM takes over from discovery.
