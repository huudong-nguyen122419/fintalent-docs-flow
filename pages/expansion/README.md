## What each activity means

### `10.01` Monitor client satisfaction and expansion opportunities during delivery

Use defined check-ins and ongoing client contact to review delivery progress, satisfaction, potential extensions, adjacent workstreams and new stakeholder needs.

**Automation target:** Automated check-in reminders, briefing and opportunity signals

### `10.02` Conduct project-completion follow-up

Ask about further projects, upcoming transactions, transformation initiatives and continued support once the engagement concludes.

**Automation target:** Automated follow-up trigger

### `10.03` Develop portfolio and account opportunities

Identify opportunities across portfolio companies, business units and related stakeholders, and act on relevant signals such as acquisitions, new funds, CFO changes or job postings.

**Automation target:** Relationship mapping, signal alerts and outreach drafting  
**Note:** KAM owns the opportunity; SDR supports research, contact identification and campaign execution.

### `10.04` Develop advocacy and track repeat business

Coordinate referrals and testimonials and maintain visibility on repeat projects and account growth.

**Automation target:** Automated referral prompts and account reporting
