## What each activity means

### `0.01` Define and maintain company type

Set the standard company classifications (Sponsor, PortCo, Corporate) used for targeting, reporting and ownership.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted classification  
**Note:** Standardization layer for the CRM. PortCo, Sponsors, Corporates

### `0.02` Clean and deduplicate company records

Remove duplicates and correct incomplete, inconsistent or outdated company data.

**Automation target:** Automated deduplication and enrichment

### `0.03` Validate company ownership and account assignment

Ensure each account has a clear owner and the correct team, portfolio and relationship links.

**Automation target:** Workflow rules and duplicate-owner flags

### `0.04` Refresh& Update, Identify and define contact roles and decision-maker taxonomy

Define the standardized functions, seniority levels and buying-process roles used to classify and prioritize contacts.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted classification  
**Note:** This defines contact types; selecting the actual person to approach remains part of Prospecting.

### `0.05` Clean and validate contact records

Validate contact details, title, employer and company relationship, and correct incomplete, outdated or incorrectly linked records.

**Automation target:** Automated enrichment, email validation and relationship checks

### `0.06` Maintain signals and trigger taxonomy

Define and maintain the commercial signals used for prospecting and expansion.

**Automation target:** Automated signal capture  
**Note:** Examples: acquisitions, carve-outs, fundraising, CFO changes and job postings.

### `0.07` Define talent taxonomy and profile data standards

Maintain the classifications required for matching, sourcing and candidate comparison.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted tagging with human governance

### `0.08` Review, approve new talent profiles , manually check & allocate to company

Approve or reject new talent profiles and correct material classification issues.

**Automation target:** AI first pass and human approval  
**Note:** Senior Ops handles exceptions and quality control.

### `0.09` Maintain revenue operations standards and governance

Maintain Standard Operating Procedure (SOP), templates, playbooks and internal guidance, and ensure pipeline data, activity records, KPI definitions, shared-inbox rules and response Service Level Agreement (SLA) remain standardized and current.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted documentation, dashboards, routing and alerts

### `0.10` Manage revenue pipeline and work queues

Review active opportunities, searches, selections, contracts and engagements; identify blockers and ensure each item progresses toward its next milestone.

### `0.11` Review and manage operational communications

Review, categorize and respond to client, talent, applicant and support messages, and escalate issues where required.
