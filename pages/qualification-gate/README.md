## What each activity means

### `3.01` Validate mandate and decision authority

Confirm that external consultants are approved and that the relevant decision-maker, influencers and signatory are identified.

**Automation target:** Qualification checklist and relationship mapping  
**Note:** Hard stop: no mandate, no external-consultant approval or no identifiable decision-maker.

### `3.02` Assess commercial and operational readiness

Validate budget, start date, commitment, work setup, procurement requirements and vendor-onboarding readiness.

**Automation target:** Completeness, budget and readiness checks  
**Note:** No budget clarity means continued qualification, not sourcing. Identify vendor-onboarding requirements early.

### `3.03` Assess delivery confidence and prioritize opportunity

Evaluate whether Fintalent can realistically fill the project and score the opportunity based on mandate, budget, urgency, relationship, attractiveness and delivery confidence.

**Automation target:** Talent-pool coverage analysis and automated opportunity scoring  
**Note:** Automated scoring supports, but does not replace, the commercial decision.

### `3.04` Apply qualification gate and decide next action

Decide whether to proceed, continue qualification, nurture or reject, and record the outcome in the pipeline.

**Automation target:** Workflow gate and status automation  
**Note:** Do not launch exploratory or pitch-dependent projects.
