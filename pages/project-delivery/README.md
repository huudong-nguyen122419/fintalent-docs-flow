## What each activity means

### `9.01` Confirm kickoff readiness and project start

Verify project start

**Automation target:** Kickoff checklist and alerts

### `9.02` Monitor engagement health and milestone progress

Track delivery, responsiveness, upcoming milestones and risks across active projects.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted project health monitoring

### `9.03` Manage client and talent communication cadence

Maintain proactive, professional communication and keep both sides aligned throughout delivery.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted communication  
**Note:** KAM is the voice of the client in the mid-term model.

### `9.04` Resolve issues, escalations and disputes

Handle operational issues, complaints and high-risk situations with clear escalation levels.

**Automation target:** Review of Time Sheet should be manually done  
**Note:** Junior Ops handles first level; Senior Ops owns escalations.

### `9.05` Monitor timesheets and delivery records

Review submitted hours or days, identify anomalies and ensure records support accurate invoicing.

**Automation target:** Automated exception detection

### `9.06` Review and issue talent-triggered client invoices

Review invoices initiated by talents against the contract, timesheets and delivery records, resolve discrepancies, and issue the approved invoices to clients.

**Automation target:** Talent-triggered invoicing, automated validation and invoice preparation  
**Note:** Talent initiates; Operations performs QA and formally issues the client invoice.

### `9.07` Monitor receivables and collect overdue payments

Track outstanding invoices, follow up on due and overdue balances and coordinate with client AP teams.

**Automation target:** Automated reminders and escalation rules

### `9.08` Reconcile receipts and execute talent payouts

Match incoming client payments and release accurate talent payments through the payout workflow.

**Automation target:** Automated reconciliation and payout preparation  
**Note:** Four-eyes approval remains mandatory.

### `9.09` Manage scope changes, extensions and amendments

Confirm changed requirements or duration and update commercial and contractual records.

**Automation target:** Change-order workflow and reminders

### `9.10` Complete project closure, feedback and offboarding

Close the engagement, collect feedback, finalize records and complete client and talent offboarding.

**Automation target:** Automated closure workflow

### `9.11` Capture product feedback and process improvements

Record bugs, friction points and improvement ideas arising from real revenue and delivery workflows.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted capture and categorization
