## What each activity means

### `7.01` Support interview scheduling and logistics

Interviews are initiated through the Fintalent chat tool. Monitor scheduling progress and intervene where coordination support is required.

**Automation target:** Chat-based scheduling workflow and automated reminders

### `7.02` Prepare candidates for interviews

Provide candidates with the relevant project context, client expectations and practical information required for the interview.

**Automation target:** Automated briefing packs

### `7.03` Track interviews, feedback and compliance checks

Monitor interview completion, capture client and candidate feedback, and review the results of the automated compliance check.

**Automation target:** Automated reminders, structured feedback and compliance screening

### `7.04` Manage follow-ups and remove decision blockers

Drive the process toward a decision and resolve unanswered questions or delays.

**Automation target:** Follow-up alerts and response drafting

### `7.05` Coordinate further interviews or checks

Arrange additional meetings, references or validation steps when genuinely required.

**Automation target:** Workflow and scheduling automation

### `7.06` Align finalist on scope, timing and fees

Confirm the final candidate's commitment, commercial terms and understanding of the engagement.

**Automation target:** Term summary and discrepancy flags

### `7.07` Review aging selection processes

Identify stalled projects and decide the next action, escalation or closure path.

**Automation target:** Aging alerts and recommended actions
