## What each activity means

### `6.01` Review incoming applications and profile quality

Perform the first-pass review of applications, CVs and material profile information.

**Automation target:** AI first-pass review

### `6.02` Validate candidate eligibility and project fit

Confirm status, capacity, compliance readiness, availability, commitment, location and rate; assess the candidate against the project pillars and must-haves; and clarify missing or inconsistent information where necessary.

**Automation target:** Automated eligibility checks, clarification prompts and fit assessment

### `6.03` Create shortlist and recommendation rationale

Review the automatically scored and ranked candidate pool, apply operational judgment and prepare a concise shortlist explaining why each candidate is relevant.

**Automation target:** Automatic scoring and ranking with AI-assisted shortlist drafting  
**Note:** System ranking informs, but does not replace, final operational judgment.

### `6.04` Approve candidate presentation

Perform the final commercial and quality review before candidates are shown to the client.

**Automation target:** Automated QA checks  
**Note:** Primary client-facing quality gate.

### `6.05` Present candidates and request client feedback

Share the shortlist, highlight the strongest options and define the expected feedback timeline.

**Automation target:** Presentation and follow-up assistance

### `6.06` Refine search and manage applicant communication

Act on client feedback, update the search and keep applicants appropriately informed.

**Automation target:** Automated candidate communications
