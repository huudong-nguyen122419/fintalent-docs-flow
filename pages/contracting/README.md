## What each activity means

### `8.01` Prepare commercial summary and confirm final terms

Summarize scope, timing, commitment, billing and pay rates, payment terms and special conditions.

**Automation target:** Automated commercial summary

### `8.02` Collect and validate client onboarding data

Obtain legal entity, billing address, tax, signatory and accounts-payable information.

**Automation target:** Guided onboarding and validation

### `8.03` Collect and validate talent onboarding data

Obtain legal, tax, banking and payment information required for contracting and payout.

**Automation target:** Guided onboarding and validation

### `8.04` Complete vendor onboarding requirements, if applicable

Register Fintalent in the client's procurement or supplier system and track the required approvals where vendor onboarding is necessary.

**Automation target:** Checklist and status tracking  
**Note:** Conditional activity. The requirement should already have been identified during Discovery and Qualification.

### `8.05` Draft client and talent contracts

Generate both agreements from the confirmed commercial and onboarding data.

**Automation target:** Automated contract generation

### `8.06` Review non-standard terms and contractual risks

Identify, normalize and approve deviations in clauses, payment terms or operational obligations.

**Automation target:** Clause extraction and risk flags  
**Note:** Payment terms above 30 days require approval; avoid 40+ days where possible.

### `8.07` Perform four-eyes contract QA

Verify parties, scope, dates, rates, payment terms, billing information and source documents before sending.

**Automation target:** Automated field comparison  
**Note:** Independent review remains mandatory.

### `8.08` Send contracts and manage signature follow-up

Issue contracts to the correct signatories and follow up until both parties have signed.

**Automation target:** Automated signature workflow and reminders

### `8.09` Activate project and complete onboarding handover

Start the contract, enable required access and establish post-signing communication channels.

**Automation target:** Workflow-driven activation

### `8.10` Notify other candidates and record outcome

Inform remaining candidates and record the won or lost reason for reporting and learning.

**Automation target:** Automated closure messages and reason capture
