## What each activity means

### `2.01` Prepare discovery call

Review the account, relationship, trigger and relevant talent-pool and market information before the call.

**Automation target:** Human interaction desired

### `2.02` Conduct discovery call and document next steps

Lead the conversation, capture the opportunity and agree the immediate follow-up.

**Automation target:** AI transcription and structured notes

### `2.03` Clarify business problem, objective and urgency

Understand why the project exists, why it matters now and what outcome the client needs.

**Automation target:** AI-assisted note structuring

### `2.04` Define project, profile and commercial requirements

Confirm scope and deliverables; required expertise, industry experience, seniority and must-haves; start date, duration and commitment; work setup, geography, travel and language requirements; budget, billing model and rate expectations; and the decision-making, signing, vendor-onboarding and payment process.

**Automation target:** Structured discovery form, AI-assisted extraction and risk flags  
**Note:** All fields must be addressed. Ask larger organizations actively about procurement and accounts-payable terms.
