## What each activity means

### `5.01` Create invitation list

Build and review the invitation pool using AI-based matching, platform filters, manual research and operational judgment.

**Automation target:** AI-based matching and recommendations  
**Note:** Automation builds the initial pool; Junior Ops remains responsible for the final invitation list.

### `5.02` Execute platform invitations

Send targeted invitations and ensure the project reaches an adequate relevant talent pool.

**Automation target:** Automated invitation dispatch  
**Note:** 80–100 invitations are indicative; candidate quality is the actual output.

### `5.03` Activate extended sourcing channels when approved

Use the existing network, referrals, organic outreach or paid sourcing selectively when Senior Ops determines that the standard platform search is insufficient.

**Automation target:** Relationship suggestions, campaign drafting and channel support  
**Note:** Conditional activity. Senior Ops approves and coordinates the extended search.

### `5.04` Monitor invitation and application response

Track invitations, responses and application volume against the project requirements and timeline.

**Automation target:** Automated monitoring and alerts

### `5.05` Perform initial talent-project plausibility check

Confirm that interested talents broadly match the project and that availability, rates, location, capacity and eligibility are plausible.

**Automation target:** Automated profile, availability and eligibility checks  
**Note:** This is an initial plausibility filter; detailed fit assessment belongs in Shortlisting.

### `5.06` Adjust or escalate sourcing when necessary

Refine filters, invitation groups or sourcing channels, or escalate low-response and hard-to-fill searches when the standard sourcing process is not producing the required candidate pool.

**Automation target:** AI performance recommendations and automated risk alerts  
**Note:** Conditional activity.
