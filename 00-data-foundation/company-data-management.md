# Company Data Management

> **0. Data Foundation** &nbsp;·&nbsp; 3 activities &nbsp;·&nbsp; flows not written yet

Everything that keeps a company record trustworthy: what the classifications mean, whether the record is a duplicate, and who owns the account.

## Why these belong together

One object, one screen. Somebody opening a company record does all three without thinking of them as separate jobs — check the type, check it is not a duplicate, check the owner is right. All three are the SDR's today.

**What still differs.** Where each is heading. `0.01` moves to Senior Ops as a governance call, `0.03` moves there too because account ownership is a judgment rather than a lookup, and `0.02` is the one piece handed to automation outright.
