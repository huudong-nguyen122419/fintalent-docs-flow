# Run the daily operation

> **0. Data Foundation** &nbsp;·&nbsp; 3 activities &nbsp;·&nbsp; steps not written yet

Keep the operation itself in working order: the procedures people follow, the queue of work in flight, and the messages coming in.

## Why these are one flow

None of the three maintains company, contact or talent data — they maintain the way the work runs. They are grouped here because they share that, and because they are the standing background tasks rather than anything triggered by a specific record.

## Worth knowing

Worth knowing while reading: these three are the only activities in Data Foundation whose reach is not limited to this stage. `0.10` covers work queues from Prospecting through Delivery, and `0.11` covers messages from clients, talents, applicants and support alike.
