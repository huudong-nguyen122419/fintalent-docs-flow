# Define and maintain a classification

> **0. Data Foundation** &nbsp;·&nbsp; 4 activities &nbsp;·&nbsp; steps not written yet

Agree what the standard values are for one kind of thing, write them down, and keep them current as reality moves.

## Why these are one flow

These four are the same act performed on four objects — companies, contacts, signals and talent. Three of them carry a byte-identical ownership signature in the matrix (SDR owns it in the short term, Senior Ops takes it over, KAM and Automation assist), and all four head toward AI-assisted classification. Writing four separate flows would produce four copies of the same steps.

**What still differs.** The object differs, and with it who owns it: talent taxonomy (`0.07`) sits with Ops from the start rather than moving there later.
